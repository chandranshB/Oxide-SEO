Think of the Site Audit feature as a gentle health checkup for your website. Oxide-SEO will look through your pages to ensure everything is working smoothly and no links are broken.

## Running Your First Checkup

1. Open the **Site Audit** tab in the app.
2. Type in your website's address (like `https://example.com`).
3. Choose how deep you want to look (default is usually perfect!).
4. Click **Start Checkup**.

## What We Look For
- **Broken Links**: We'll find any pages that say "404 Not Found" so you can fix them.
- **Missing Titles**: We make sure every page has a clear title and description so people can find you easily on Google.
- **Speed Bumps**: We check if any of your pages are taking too long to load.

## Performance & Concurrency

The site crawler is engineered for speed and efficiency. Powered by a highly concurrent backend, it handles asynchronous requests without the overhead of heavy headless browsers. This allows the scanner to process thousands of pages per minute on standard consumer hardware, with configurable thread limits so you can tailor the intensity to your network's capacity.
