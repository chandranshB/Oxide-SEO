Competitor Watch helps you learn from others and discover new opportunities to shine brighter in search results.

## How It Helps
- **Find the Missing Pieces**: Discover the search terms your competitors are showing up for, but you aren't yet!
- **Track New Ideas**: See when they publish new pages so you can keep your own content fresh and competitive.
- **Link Watching**: Gently monitor who is linking to them, giving you ideas for your own outreach.

## Analytical Approach

Rather than relying on expensive third-party scrapers that charge per credit, the application utilizes localized comparison algorithms. By leveraging your own machine's processing power to cross-reference domain footprints, it delivers real-time strategic insights without the recurring costs typically associated with enterprise-level competitor tracking.
