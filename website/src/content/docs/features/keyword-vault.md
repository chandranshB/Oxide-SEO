The Keyword Vault is your personal, ultra-fast notebook for keyword research.

## What Can You Do?
- **Understand the "Why"**: Easily group keywords based on what people are actually looking for (like learning something new vs. buying a product).
- **See the Numbers**: Check how many people are searching for specific terms and how hard it might be to rank for them.
- **Stay Organized**: Add colorful tags and filter through thousands of words instantly without your computer slowing down.

## Local Data Handling

Handling massive keyword lists can cause traditional web applications to lag or freeze. The Keyword Vault circumvents this by leveraging highly optimized local data structures. Searching, filtering, and mass-updating thousands of keywords happens instantaneously right on your device, eliminating the need for premium cloud storage tiers or constant internet connectivity.
